# Chapter Two: approved direction

Approved 2026-11-08. This document records narrative and exploration direction only. Chapter Two is not implemented, and this is not yet a build-ready playable-sequence plan. No chapter subtitle is selected.

## Emotional progression

Daniel's death changes Thomas's search from looking for anyone to looking for the people he remembers. He seeks his family home because someone might have returned there, or left evidence of what happened. He knows Daniel died; he does not know the fate of all the citizens or his parents.

Give the loss room before introducing hope. Grace's possible survival offers a reason to continue without resolving Thomas's grief or undoing his losses. Neither parent's death is confirmed in this chapter; those discoveries occur separately later.

## Continuity and presentation

- Preserve Chapter One's completed Daniel discovery, earlier identity recovery, and lack of a Grace survival reveal. Do not retroactively turn its ending into a live signal.
- Grace is alive as author knowledge, but Thomas and the shadow must discover evidence. The shadow has no special access to her current condition or whereabouts.
- Thomas and Grace survived by luck. Do not introduce special protection, connected survival actions, or a supernatural hunter to explain that fact.
- Let Grace's decisions affect the route. She has her own agency, rather than serving only as a reward at the end of Thomas's grief.
- Keep pursuit and survival environmental unless a new threat is explicitly approved. Reuse forgiving movement and explicit safe-checkpoint retry. Specific encounters and their number are not selected yet.
- Required discoveries must establish the relationship and final lead; optional rooms enrich them. Preserve exploration without requiring every branch for essential understanding.
